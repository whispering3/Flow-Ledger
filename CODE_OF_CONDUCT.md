# Code of Conduct

We want FlowLedger to be a welcoming, professional project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Respect privacy and never share credentials or private customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized language, and the disclosure of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
